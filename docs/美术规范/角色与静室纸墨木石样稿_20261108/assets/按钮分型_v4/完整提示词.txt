=== primary ===
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: one production-ready transparent raster UI control base for a Chinese cultivation game, no text, no symbol.
Reference images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: match their restrained hand-painted matte grey-brown wood, warm ivory handmade paper, grey-blue cloth/stone, dark ink-like outlines. Do not copy the scene, architecture, shelves, labels or button shapes. Coordinate palette: paper #EFE7D6, light paper #F5EFE2, muted wood #70615A, slate #687681, accent #526D60. Finish must look crafted and understated, not shiny, not gold, not a modern glossy app.
OUTPUT: a single isolated complete control centered on a genuinely transparent RGBA canvas. Outside the exact silhouette must be alpha 0, including every corner and cut-out. No surrounding wall/wood/paper rectangle, no checkerboard painted into the image, no grey or white backdrop, no detached flecks, no border halo, no external cast shadow, no blurred transparent dust. Keep all depth and shading INSIDE the silhouette. Clean anti-aliased edge.
Composition: orthographic front view, horizontal and perfectly symmetric in height, straight parallel edges, no perspective. Center is calm and almost flat for live dark text. A little handmade texture, not grunge or high contrast. Do NOT include letters, words, Chinese characters, icons, logos, numbers or watermarks. Do not create a sheet of options; exactly one complete control.
Specific control design: A broad main action button, aspect ratio 6:1, approximately 1200x200 visible shape on a landscape canvas. Mostly warm ivory paper face (85% of height), narrow grey-brown wooden top and sides, a purposeful dark pine-grey-green pressed wooden lower lip (last 12% height) with a tiny light rim. Very subtly clipped straight corners, no concave ornamental corners. This is a deliberate strong action control, refined with clear physical thickness, heavier than a regular button but not bulky. Keep the entire central 75% width empty and readable. Outer margin at least 4% on every side.
