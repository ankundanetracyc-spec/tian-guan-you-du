Use case: ui-mockup, high fidelity Chinese xianxia PC game event window concept.
Edit the provided actual screenshot of our game 天光有渡. Preserve the original 16:9 game-screen composition and recognizable ink-wash forest/map tile backdrop, but dramatically redesign its small text-only modal into a much larger illustrated narrative-event popup. This is a finished visual concept for a real game UI, not a diagram or annotated design presentation.
The central popup occupies about 76% of screen width and 86% of screen height, with generous readable margins, fully within screen. Warm ivory xuan paper, very subtle irregular paper edges, delicate dark ink rules, restrained cinnabar details. Elegant simple Chinese book typography, all text crisp and readable. Keep the map dimmed and softly defocused around the outer edges so the event dominates.
Layout: slim header at top with small location “东林地 · 浅林” and large event title “林中遭遇”; a discreet close X at upper right. Below the header, a large beautiful wide narrative illustration occupies roughly 60% of the popup interior height. This MUST be a substantial landscape illustration, not a tiny icon or character portrait in a box. Art is handmade Chinese ink and restrained mineral watercolor on paper, matching the game's Chinese ink landscape aesthetic. Show ONE lean gray-black forest wolf emerging from gnarled roots and fern-covered forest shade into a narrow woodland trail, low tense posture, looking toward viewer, detailed fur rendered with lively ink strokes. Ancient trees, mossy stones, drifting woodland mist, subtle pale light filtering through canopy. Cinematic landscape composition, calm menace, no attack or blood, no magical glow or flashy effects. The wolf is clearly visible and about a quarter of illustration width, set within the environment. No other wolves, no characters, no enemy stats over the art. Artwork edges subtly soften into paper, no gilded frames.
Below the illustration put only two clean left-aligned narrative lines with comfortable line spacing:
“枝叶忽然一晃，一头凶狼从近处的树影中现出身形。”
“它伏低身子，目光紧盯着你。是否应战，还须掂量自身实力。”
Bottom decision area has TWO large distinct clickable horizontal buttons with refined ink borders, balanced spacing. Secondary button “暂不应战” in paper and dark ink, primary button “查看战前情报” with a muted cinnabar ink fill and ivory lettering. Make the buttons visibly large, accessible, and deliberate, around 58px high relative to a 1080px screen. Primary action only opens briefing, not immediate combat.
No costs, no rewards, no character attributes, no extra labels, no arrows or annotations, no logos, no watermark, no Photoshop chrome, no modern blue app styling, no ornate gold fantasy ornaments, no tiny font, no huge empty text area. Overall the user should immediately see a large immersive illustrated event window, with the illustration as its heart.
