# v2 简化立绘生成提示

工具：内置 imagegen。模式：编辑（style-transfer）；透明输出。男、女分别生成，原图留档。

## 男修

编辑目标：首版男修；辅助参考：当前青尾狐图鉴笔触。

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Edit target: image 1, male combat figure. Reference image 2 is the game's existing bestiary, for matte muted mineral colours and ink edges only; do not draw an animal.
Redesign this male cultivator into a SIMPLER in-game sprite readable at only 190 pixels tall. The original is too finely detailed. Keep the same adult male identity, completely blank featureless warm ivory face, deep jade green and ivory palette, charcoal tied long hair, full-body three-quarter facing SCREEN RIGHT, genuine transparent background. Dramatically reduce detail: remove ALL embroidered flowers, vines, clouds and brocade; no fabric grain, no strands of individual hair, no fine etched hatching. Hair is 5–7 large ink locks. Robe has 8–12 broad major folds, broad flat mineral-colour fills with just 2–3 restrained shadow values, occasional dry ink edge. Plain robe, one narrow muted beige cuff edge at most. Natural adult proportions about seven heads tall, modest functional sleeves instead of giant spread silk.
CHANGE POSE: grounded martial guard, torso distinctly side-on toward right, weight on slightly bent rear leg, forward foot aimed toward opponent, both soles complete and visible. One bent arm extends a relaxed open guarding palm at lower-chest height toward screen right, elbow visibly bent; other hand loosely closed near waist. Shoulders lowered, compact powerful triangular silhouette. No weapon. This must differ markedly from original two arms hanging open pose.
Face: no eyebrows, eyes, nose, mouth, beard or facial lines. Only blank skin plane, head silhouette and ears.
Output: ONE isolated complete figure, portrait canvas, generous transparent margin, all topknot and boots inside canvas. Transparent outside silhouette, NO shadow, halo, glow, ground, backdrop, UI or text. Crisp hand-painted illustrated game sprite, not an elaborate portrait painting, not a 3D model, not chibi.
```

## 女修

编辑目标：首版女修；辅助参考：本轮 v2 男修的简化程度，禁止复用男修姿势。

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Edit target image 1: female cultivator. Reference image 2: newly simplified male companion, for exactly this level of restrained ink outline and broad mineral-colour shadow planes; use ONLY its simpler rendering, NOT its martial palm-out pose.
Redesign the female for a small in-game combat sprite at 190 pixels tall, simpler and more functional. Keep blank featureless warm ivory face, charcoal half-up black long hair, slate blue and ivory plain robes, full-body three-quarter facing screen RIGHT. Remove ALL dense flower, cloud and vine embroidery. Remove individual hair filaments, etched texture and tiny folds. Five to seven broad hair locks, 8–12 main robe folds, 2–3 matte colour/shadow values, restrained imperfect ink edges, very subtle pigment texture. Plain cuffs, simple cloth sash. Not ornate, not a beauty portrait.
CHANGE POSE, strongly different from male: composed upright spellcaster stance with narrower footing, rear foot planted and front foot a half step to the right, both complete shoes visible. One forearm lifted vertically in front of upper chest with two fingers forming a small cultivation hand seal upward; elbow bent and close to torso, hand at collarbone height. Other arm sweeps gently across the lower waist, holding/collecting the draping opposite sleeve, hand partly visible. Slim vertical S-shaped silhouette, sleeves gathered rather than spread, calm controlled pose. Do NOT extend an open palm horizontally like the male. No weapon, no magic effects. Natural adult proportions about seven heads tall, no childlike body.
Head turned toward opponent RIGHT; absolutely NO eyes, brows, nose, mouth, lashes or facial markings; face is a warm skin plane. A simple hair knot with a small blue tie, no clusters of flowers/jewels.
Output ONE complete isolated figure on portrait canvas, intact head and both shoes with transparent margin all around. Genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha outside silhouette, no ground shadow, background, vignette, halo, UI or text. Match the simplified male line weight and muted mineral paint rendering.
```
