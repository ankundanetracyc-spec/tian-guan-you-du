COMMON ART DIRECTION — indoor functional interfaces for this existing Chinese cultivation RPG.
Match the project's ACTUALLY INSTALLED interior painting, especially the reference study_room.png: MATTE, low-saturation hand-painted gouache/watercolor pigment planes, broad coherent form-following brushwork, clear controlled contours, grey-blue brick and stone, desaturated grey-brown timber, muted blue-grey cloth, off-white rice paper, tiny pine-grey green selection accents, deep warm-grey ink lettering. Natural quiet indoor daylight. Keep the local colors solid and readable rather than pale washed-out.
Lighting is physically ordinary indoor diffuse daylight: absolutely NO bloom, halos, radiant paper, glowing button borders, white edge highlights, rim lights, volumetric rays, mist, atmospheric veil, glitter, stippling or floating pale dots. A subtle real contact shadow is okay. No gold ornamental UI, roof-shaped interface crowns, pagoda roofs, clouds under menus, outdoor palace frames, engraved gold filigree, giant badges or cartoon shine.
The setting is a REAL ROOM: grey-blue brick wall, timber posts and ceiling beams, modest lattice window and a visible strip of grounded floor. Cabinet feet touch the floor; boards touch cabinets, walls or the counter. Title sign is physically fastened to a beam or backboard, not hovering. Detail paper is attached to a rigid cabinet-mounted board; action area is part of the counter. Interfaces may be neatly front-facing for legibility, but all furniture has convincing joints, backing, support and contact shadows. No unsupported architecture or panels floating in a mountain sky. Outside through windows occupies at most 8–10% of the frame and remains subordinate. No outdoor roof canopy inside the room.
Consistent fine plain timber / dry-ink paper edges, not thick multilayer gilded ornamental frames. The paper is lightly textured and matte, never a light source. Dark ink text everywhere; no white text.
Typography target at a 1920x1080 design scale: main title about 36px, tabs and section headings 24px, book-spine names and body 22px, primary button 28px. Preserve relative hierarchy and readability, no giant heading that consumes usable space, no font shrinkage for individual long book names. All book spines use one fixed width, height, title-slip size and font size. Long titles fit vertically in the shared standard height. Never widen, enlarge or thicken a selected or long-title book. Selection is a quiet ink marker or pine-grey outline, no glow or enlargement.
Only show actual configured available records: no filler books, blank dummy spines, locked placeholder manuals, duplicates, made-up commodities, fake stocks, decorative books or false catalogue capacity. UI content is sparse if the actual catalogue is sparse; furniture and its closed doors fit the catalogue rather than simulating dozens of entries.
Output one polished coherent 16:9 game-interface concept screenshot. No people, dialogue, English, editorial notes, watermarks, blurred corners, vignette, 3D rendering or photorealism. This is a full new matte rendering, not a brightness filter over the old glowing concept. The primary indoor art reference overrides old interface decoration.
